What is real, measured and assumed

Real: the steps, their order, the loops, the line references, the cron every minute and the Worker's 30-second CPU limit (wrangler.jsonc:135) come from EXO's source at commit 5362780. No EXO code was run.

Measured: copies of EXO's matching functions, timed on made-up task titles in this container: 4.9 µs per identity hash, 1 µs per comparison wording, 8.2 µs per title comparison.

Assumed: 10 open tasks per person plus 40 archived per year; a third of tasks came from meetings; 20 action lines, 12 reach the duplicate check, 8 are added. 120 ms per Google file check, 600 ms to read the notes, 6 s for the Sonnet call, 350 ms per Sheet read, 5 ms per database query, 900 ms to write and read back the rows. CPU counts only matching and parsing, not waiting on Google or Sonnet. The 15-minute wait for notes to settle is left out.

Simplified: dots arrive faster as the team grows and the clock is sped up, so the crowd at each star shows where meetings spend their time. EXO itself takes one meeting per cron tick, so a real backlog would wait at the start rather than at each star.
